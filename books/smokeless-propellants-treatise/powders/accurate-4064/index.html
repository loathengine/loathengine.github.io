Compendium/Accurate· Burn Rank #063

Accurate 4064

OEM / Twin: Proprietary · GD-OTS Canada (CA)

Western Powders introduced Accurate 4064 in the 2000s, adopting the iconic 4064 designation to signal a versatile medium-burning extruded propellant positioned alongside classic .308 and .30-06 sticks. Early European sourcing drew on Explosia in the Czech Republic (parallel to Lovex S062 era lines) before production shifted to General Dynamics Ordnance and Tactical Systems Canada in Valleyfield, Quebec, built to Accurate’s own specification. Despite sharing the Valleyfield plant with IMR 4064, the two were never identical twins.

A single-base extruded powder cut slightly shorter than legacy DuPont-pattern 4064 sticks, Accurate 4064 improves metering while covering .22-250 Remington, .308 Winchester, 7mm-08 Remington, and .30-06 Springfield. In November 2019, Western Powders recalled specific Valleyfield lots manufactured prior to October 2016 due to stabilizer depletion risks that could cause autocatalytic degradation. Handloaders must verify lot codes against the official recall list and never substitute IMR 4064 charge tables for Accurate 4064.
