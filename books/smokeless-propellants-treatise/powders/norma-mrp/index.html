Compendium/Norma· Burn Rank #116

Norma MRP

Eurenco Bofors AB (Karlskoga, Sweden)

Norma MRP—an acronym for Magnum Rifle Powder—was introduced in the 1960s during Norma Precision AB’s historic collaboration with Roy Weatherby to produce factory Weatherby Magnum ammunition in Sweden. Manufactured by Eurenco Bofors AB in Karlskoga, Sweden, MRP became one of the most celebrated slow-burning sporting propellants in Europe and North America. It occupies the same Karlskoga Bofors lineage and burn-rate class as Alliant Reloder 22.

Formulated as a high-energy, single-perforated double-base extruded propellant complying with European Union REACH mandates, Norma MRP packs substantial bulk density and energetic output into overbore magnum cases. Although Norma MRP and Bofors-made Alliant Reloder 22 are closely related counterparts, Norma and Alliant maintain independent lot tolerances and specifications, so handloaders must never substitute charges 1:1 across labels. Norma MRP excels in 6.5x55mm Swedish with heavy bullets, .270 Weatherby Magnum, 7mm Remington Magnum, .300 Winchester Magnum, and .300 Weatherby Magnum.
