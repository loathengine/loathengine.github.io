Front Matter· Essential Safety Rules

Before You Begin

These rules apply every time you handle or fire a rifle. Read them before your first range session. They are repeated as a card in Appendix B.

With every firearm

  1. Treat every firearm as if it is loaded. Check the chamber yourself whenever you pick up or hand off a rifle.
  2. Keep the muzzle pointed in a safe direction at all times.
  3. Keep your finger off the trigger and outside the trigger guard until your sights are on the target and you have decided to fire.
  4. Know your target, your backstop, and what lies beyond both.

At the range and in the field

  1. Wear eye and ear protection whenever you are on an active firing line. Use double hearing protection near muzzle brakes.
  2. Use a chamber flag whenever the rifle is cased, uncased, or on a cold firing line.
  3. Confirm the rifle is clear of bore obstructions and that any suppressor is properly aligned and tight before firing.
  4. Shoot only known-good ammunition that matches the cartridge stamped on the barrel. For handloads, fire only loads that have completed a safe work-up (see The Handloading Handbook).
  5. Stop immediately at any pressure sign: stiff bolt lift, new or heavy ejector marks, or flattened, cratered, or pierced primers.
  6. Keep ammunition out of direct sun and out of a hot chamber. Do not leave a live round sitting in a hot chamber while waiting to fire.
  7. If a shot sounds or feels wrong, stop. Unload the rifle and inspect the bore for a stuck bullet before firing again. If a round fails to fire, keep the muzzle downrange for at least 30 seconds before opening the action.
  8. Wash your hands and face after shooting or handling fired brass and targets. Do not eat, drink, or smoke until you have washed.
