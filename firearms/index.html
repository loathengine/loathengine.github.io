About Empirical Precision

Empirical Precision is a free, open-source workbench for precision rifle shooters and handloaders. It turns what happens at the range into numbers you can trust, and it works fully offline.

Tools

  • Ballistics calculator: bullet drop, wind drift and time of flight for your rifle, using your bullet’s ballistic coefficient.
  • DOPE card generator: printable elevation and wind holds for the distances you shoot.
  • Bullet stability: gyroscopic stability for your bullet and barrel twist rate.
  • Monte Carlo dispersion: see how small variations in velocity, wind and aim add up to the groups you get on paper.
  • Internal ballistics: model chamber pressure and muzzle velocity as estimates for study and comparison.
  • Chronograph analysis: average velocity, standard deviation and extreme spread from your velocity strings.
  • Shot group analysis: measure mean radius, extreme spread and group size in MOA.
  • Target marking: plot bullet holes on your targets so they can be measured.
  • Printable targets: custom paper targets and calibrated sight-in grids.
  • Range session log: keep each range day’s rifle, velocities and groups together.
  • Component database: bullets, cartridges and powders with the specifications the models need.
  • Published load library: browse published load coverage by cartridge and powder across reloading manuals.
  • Powder burn rate chart: compare relative burn speeds across powders.

Use it safely

Every model here is an estimate. Confirm trajectories with real shots on paper, and never use a calculated result in place of published load data from a component manufacturer.
